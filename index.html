About NimiqSplit

NimiqSplit simplifies splitting dining bills, group outings, and shared expenses using the Nimiq blockchain.

How it works:

1. Enter your bill total and choose your currency (NIM, USD, EUR, CFA).

2. Select the group size and optional tip percentage.

3. Enter the receiver's Nimiq address (or connect your wallet).

4. Pay directly with the Nimiq Hub or show the QR code to your friends!

Fast, browser-native transactions powered by Nimiq.

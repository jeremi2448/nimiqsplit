About NimiqSplit

NimiqSplit is a decentralized bill-splitting mini app running natively on the Nimiq network.

How Payments Work:

  • Direct Wallet Connect: Connect your Nimiq Hub/Safe wallet in one click using the "Connect Wallet" button at the top.
  • Instant Checkout: Click "Pay via Wallet" to sign and broadcast the transaction directly through Nimiq Hub on Mainnet.
  • Mobile App Deep-Link: Settle instantly in the Nimiq Pay mobile app via URI scheme.
  • QR Code: Present the QR code to friends so they can scan and pay their exact share from any crypto wallet.
  • Live CoinGecko Rates: Automatic conversion between NIM and USDT/USD.

Step-by-step:

  1. Tap Connect Wallet to auto-fill your recipient address (or paste your Nimiq Pay address).
  2. Enter total bill and choose currency (NIM or USDT).
  3. Select how many people to split with.
  4. Tap Pay via Wallet to trigger the transaction, or Show QR for your friends to scan!

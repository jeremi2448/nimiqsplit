About NimiqSplit

NimiqSplit is a decentralized, instant bill-splitting mini application running natively on the Nimiq network.

Key Features:

  • Real-time CoinGecko Rates: Fetches live NIM/USDT exchange rates on every split.
  • Dual Currency: Split directly in NIM or USDT equivalent.
  • Direct Wallet Deep-Links: Open directly in Nimiq Pay or Safe using standard Nimiq URI schemes.
  • Instant QR Code Generator: Let friends scan and settle their share on their own devices.
  • Local History: Keep track of your latest group payments locally.

How to test on Mainnet:

  1. Install Nimiq Pay on your smartphone.
  2. Paste your Nimiq address in the Recipient field.
  3. Enter total bill and tap "Pay in App" or "Show QR".
  4. Nimiq Pay handles the instant settlement on Mainnet.
